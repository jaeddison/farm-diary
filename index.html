Paddocks out of the round

▾
For crop, silage or anything else taken out. Set the date, then tap a paddock to take it out from that date; tap a red one to put it back from that date. Round length uses the paddocks in the round on each day.

Break sizes

▾
Planned area (ha) for each herd's AM and PM break. Pick the date a change takes effect from; days before it keep whatever applied then (or nothing). On the Today page each break shows how much is left and fills the next paddock with the remainder.

▾
